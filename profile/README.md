# MeWeBe: the startup studio that takes less and shares more

We build MVPs for founders using AI tooling, with human engineering review on every build. Founders choose a route: we build free and take 3-7% equity (Grant / Equity Partner), or they fund a cut-down build and keep 100% ownership (Funded Build). Every startup pledges a share of future profits (recommended 5-20%) to a community pool. Applications get a reply within 5-7 working days, either way.

**Me. We. Be.** We all contribute, we all benefit.

## The free founder league

Join free at [mewebe.com/register](https://mewebe.com/register?utm_source=github&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=org_readme). Members start with 500 MWB Coins (no cash value today) and earn more for streaks, quests, forum posts and referrals, backing startups in a fantasy startup league. Coin payouts are a roadmap goal, not a promise; we share updates openly as milestones ship.
